## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the sophistication and deliberate pacing of high-end independent food publications and literary gastronomic journals. It is crafted for discerning culinary enthusiasts, home epicures, and thoughtful food critics who seek curated depth over rapid consumption.

The visual style blends refined editorial minimalism with classical typographic discipline. Generous white space, architectural margins, and deliberate typographic contrast create an environment of quiet confidence. Instead of overwhelming the user with loud badges or transactional density, the interface acts as an uncluttered canvas that honors food imagery and editorial writing. The emotional atmosphere is cultured, calm, appetizing, and authoritative.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the culinary seed accent `#E05344`, an organic terracotta-coral that evokes vine-ripened heritage tomatoes, pimentón, and warm embers. Used with surgical discretion, it signifies primary calls to action, bookmark states, and editorial highlights without competing with visual food photography.

Secondary accents in deep culinary slate `#2C3E50` anchor interactive navigation, structural lines, and selected filter chips, while a warm artisan wheat tertiary tone `#D4A373` offers subtle support for badges and culinary tags. 

Backgrounds rely on a calm, warm ivory-tinted off-white canvas `#FDFBF7`, framed by pure white `#FFFFFF` editorial containers and delicate border dividers in `#EDE8E1`. Typography utilizes neutral charcoal `#1E2022` for high-contrast, effortless legibility, softened by secondary muted slate-taupe `#68645E` for metadata, dates, and bylines.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between the literary heritage of *Newsreader* and the functional geometric discipline of *Manrope*. 

Headlines harness the expressive serifs, optical sizing, and organic curves of *Newsreader* to deliver a magazine masthead aura. Editorial subheadings and culinary titles evoke warmth and literary credibility.

Body and interactive text leverage *Manrope*, providing neutral clarity, open counters, and high legibility across recipe cards, dietary indices, and navigation controls. Letter spacing in uppercase micro-labels is tracked out (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to preserve crystalline legibility at compact scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an asymmetric 12-column editorial grid for desktop and an adaptable 4-column structure for mobile viewports. Layouts prioritize breathing room, utilizing substantial canvas margins and deliberate negative space to create a gallery feel.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max layout width capped at 1440px, centered with 48px (`3rem`) side margins and 24px (`1.5rem`) column gutters. Wide columns host featured essays and photographic spreads, while 3-column spans host curated dish cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, 32px side margins, 16px gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, 20px (`1.25rem`) edge margins, 16px gutters.

Vertical rhythm is strictly orchestrated around multiples of 8px. Spacing between major content sections relies on generous empty territory (`space-xl` and above) to naturally delineate content without overusing horizontal dividing rules.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows and faux-3D layering in favor of an authentic flat editorial aesthetic punctuated by whisper-soft ambient warmth.

Depth is achieved through **tonal layering** and **fine hairline boundaries**:
- **Canvas Base:** `#FDFBF7` provides a tactile, non-glare surface.
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Crisp pure white `#FFFFFF` sits cleanly on top of the warm canvas, separated by hairline `1px` borders in `#EDE8E1`.
- **Floating Overlays & Popovers:** For contextual menus and flyout recipe filters, depth is conveyed using an ultra-diffused ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.05)`, backed by a `1px` solid outline in `#E5DFD7`.

Interactive states rely on sharp micro-transitions—such as border color shifts or subtle background warmth shifts—rather than heavy physical elevation leaps.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is governed by soft architectural restraint (`roundedness: 1`). Corner radii are subtle, avoiding playful pill motifs in favor of refined, book-like precision:

- Standard controls, text inputs, and interactive chips use `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- Curated dish cards, culinary photography frames, and modal panels use `rounded-lg` at `0.5rem` (8px).
- Extended feature sheets and large hero banners use `rounded-xl` at `0.75rem` (12px).

Photography retains crisp structural geometry, honoring traditional print magazine layouts with clean rectangular crops that harmonize with the gentle curve of interactive containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#E05344`, text in `#FFFFFF`, with `0.25rem` corner radius and `10px 20px` padding. Hover transitions to `#CA4335`. No shadow; depth is pure flat color authority.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, `1px` border in `#2C3E50`, label in `#2C3E50`. Hover triggers subtle background fill of `rgba(44, 62, 80, 0.04)`.
- **Editorial Text Button:** Underlined label in `#1E2022` with a `1px` baseline offset and an interactive arrow glyph (`→`) that shifts `4px` on hover.

### Recipe & Curation Cards
- White `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a single `1px` solid `#EDE8E1` perimeter.
- Top section holds photographic content with a fixed 4:3 or 16:10 aspect ratio and an 8px (`rounded-lg`) top radius.
- Padding inside the card content block is `space-lg` (24px).
- Metadata strings (prep time, region, provenance) are styled in `label-md` uppercase with `#68645E`.

### Filter Chips
- Unselected chips feature a light canvas fill `#F5F2EC`, neutral text `#68645E`, and a `1px` border in `#E5DFD7`.
- Selected chips shift to solid `#2C3E50` with `#FFFFFF` text, maintaining a crisp `4px` corner radius.

### Input Fields & Search
- Inputs use `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` solid border `#EDE8E1`, and `12px 16px` internal padding.
- Placeholder copy is set in *Newsreader* italic `#948E85` to simulate curated search prompts (e.g., *"Search ingredients, seasonal harvests, or tasting notes..."*).
- Focus states replace the border with `#E05344` without an outer glow.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16px boxes with `2px` corners. Checked state fills with `#E05344` and a white check icon.
- Radio indicators feature an outer 16px circle with a centered 6px dot in `#E05344` on selection.

### Editorial Byline & Pull-Quote Components
- Distinctive to this culinary publication system: pull-quotes feature a 2px left border in `#E05344`, 24px left padding, and *Newsreader* italic text in `headline-md`.
